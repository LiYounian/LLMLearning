参考文献与来源

本课每一条涉及公式 / 模型 / 数字的结论都尽量对应一手来源：论文（arXiv / ACL / ICML）、官方技术报告、代码仓库。存疑或社区起源处已在正文与下方显式标注，不臆造。

核心方法论文

  1. [Sinusoidal / Transformer] Vaswani et al. (2017). Attention Is All You Need. NeurIPS. arXiv:1706.03762
  2. [Learned absolute / BERT] Devlin et al. (2018/2019). BERT: Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers. NAACL. arXiv:1810.04805
  3. [Learned absolute / GPT-2] Radford et al. (2019). Language Models are Unsupervised Multitask Learners. OpenAI PDF
  4. [Learned absolute / ViT] Dosovitskiy et al. (2020). An Image is Worth 16x16 Words. ICLR 2021. arXiv:2010.11929
  5. [Relative / Shaw] Shaw, Uszkoreit, Vaswani (2018). Self-Attention with Relative Position Representations. NAACL. arXiv:1803.02155
  6. [Relative / Transformer-XL] Dai et al. (2019). Transformer-XL: Attentive Language Models Beyond a Fixed-Length Context. ACL. arXiv:1901.02860
  7. [Relative / DeBERTa] He et al. (2020). DeBERTa: Decoding-enhanced BERT with Disentangled Attention. ICLR 2021. arXiv:2006.03654
  8. [T5 相对位置偏置] Raffel et al. (2019/2020). Exploring the Limits of Transfer Learning with a Unified Text-to-Text Transformer (T5). JMLR. arXiv:1910.10683
  9. [RoPE] Su et al. (2021). RoFormer: Enhanced Transformer with Rotary Position Embedding. arXiv:2104.09864
  10. [ALiBi] Press, Smith, Lewis (2021). Train Short, Test Long: Attention with Linear Biases Enables Input Length Extrapolation. ICLR 2022. arXiv:2108.12409
  11. [NoPE] Kazemnejad et al. (2023). The Impact of Positional Encoding on Length Generalization in Transformers. NeurIPS. arXiv:2305.19466

长上下文外推

  1. [位置插值 PI] Chen et al. (2023). Extending Context Window of Large Language Models via Positional Interpolation. arXiv:2306.15595
  2. [NTK-aware / Dynamic-NTK] 社区起源、非正式论文：Reddit 用户 u/bloc97（NTK-aware Scaled RoPE）、u/emozilla（Dynamic-NTK），早期 PI 微调实践 kaiokendev。后被 Hugging Face transformers（v4.31 起）收录为 RoPE scaling 选项。原始讨论帖
  3. [YaRN] Peng, Quesnelle, Fan, Shippole (2023). YaRN: Efficient Context Window Extension of Large Language Models. ICLR 2024. arXiv:2309.00071 · 代码
  4. [LongRoPE] Ding, Zhang et al. (2024). LongRoPE: Extending LLM Context Window Beyond 2 Million Tokens. ICML 2024（集成于 Phi-3）。arXiv:2402.13753 · 代码

主流大模型技术报告（位置编码采用核对）

  1. [LLaMA] Touvron et al. (2023). LLaMA / Llama 2. arXiv:2302.13971 · arXiv:2307.09288（RoPE）
  2. [Qwen] Bai et al. (2023). Qwen Technical Report. arXiv:2309.16609（RoPE + 动态 NTK / 长文本方案）
  3. [DeepSeek-V2] DeepSeek-AI (2024). DeepSeek-V2（MLA 与 decoupled RoPE）。arXiv:2405.04434
  4. [BLOOM] BigScience (2022). BLOOM: A 176B-Parameter Open-Access Multilingual LM（ALiBi）。arXiv:2211.05100
  5. [MPT] MosaicML (2023). MPT-7B / Introducing MPT（ALiBi）。MosaicML 博客
  6. [Falcon] Almazrouei et al. (2023). The Falcon Series of Open Language Models（团队实测选 RoPE 而非 ALiBi）。arXiv:2311.16867
  7. [GLM/ChatGLM] Du et al. (2022). GLM: General Language Model Pretraining（2D RoPE 谱系）。arXiv:2103.10360
⚠️ 关于准确性与时效
位置编码本身相对稳定，但各模型「上下文长度 / 具体外推方案」随版本迭代变化很快，本课表格取代表值，确切数字请以对应官方发布为准。NTK-aware 这类社区起源、无正式论文的方法已显式标注。若发现与最新官方口径不一致，以官方为准。
